Stage 01 of 06

Lead arrives

Read-only intake

What happens

Accepts an inbound lead from a web form webhook or equivalent intake trigger, then normalizes contact fields, source attributes, and timestamps for downstream steps.

Controls & safeguards

Pack is imported inactive in n8n. No live form submissions are processed until you wire the intake endpoint and activate the workflow. No external message is sent here.

Interfaces

  • n8n webhook
  • Form intake

Next: Normalized payload passes to CRM dedupe.

Stage 02 of 06

Dedupe and upsert

Gated CRM write

What happens

Looks up an existing contact before writing to prevent duplicates, then creates or updates the record in HubSpot (with optional HighLevel or Sheets mapping) and assigns a lead owner.

Controls & safeguards

CRM write is gated by dedupe lookup and explicit credentials. Because the workflow is imported inactive and defaults to testMode, field mappings can be verified before live use.

Interfaces

  • HubSpot
  • HighLevel (optional)
  • Sheets (optional)

Next: Contact ID and owner pass to internal alert.

Stage 03 of 06

Owner alert

Owner-only notification

What happens

Sends an internal notification to the assigned owner with the lead's intake summary and CRM record reference so a human can review the inquiry immediately.

Controls & safeguards

Internal owner-only path: the lead is not contacted at this stage. Safe to inspect during initial setup under the testMode default before any customer-facing channel is enabled.

Interfaces

  • Slack / Email
  • Internal owner channel

Next: Outbound eligibility checked at stage 04.

Stage 04 of 06

Consent, quiet hours, and STOP gates

Gated compliance check

What happens

Verifies documented outreach consent, checks the configured quiet-hours schedule, and confirms the contact has not sent a STOP or opt-out signal.

Controls & safeguards

No outbound message proceeds without all three checks passing. Missing consent, active quiet hours, or a recorded STOP halts dispatch on this branch.

Gate checks

  • Consent flag
  • Quiet hours
  • STOP / opt-out

Next: Eligible records enter the testMode gate.

Stage 05 of 06

testMode preview vs live send

Human approval / hard stop

What happens

Branches outbound delivery on the testMode setting. By default, testMode is on: the workflow generates [TEST] previews internally and never messages the lead.

Controls & safeguards

Hard stop before the first live message to a real lead. Turning off testMode is an explicit human go-live decision after reviewing [TEST] previews and consent, quiet hours, and STOP gates.

Default state

  • Imported inactive
  • testMode: on
  • [TEST] previews

Next: Live send only after human go-live.

Stage 06 of 06

First reply and follow-ups

Gated external send

What happens

Dispatches the initial response to the lead and runs configured follow-up steps while logging activity back to the contact record.

Controls & safeguards

Reached only after the workflow is activated from imported inactive, cleared past the hard stop (testMode off), and approved by consent, quiet hours, and STOP gates.

Interfaces

  • Gmail / Email
  • SMS (if enabled)
  • Follow-up cadence

Stop condition: Reply or STOP halts follow-ups.

Defaults: imported inactive · testMode = true ([TEST] preview) · hard stop before first live message
